How do I find a mentor?
Go to the Find Mentor tab and browse by skill, industry, or location. You can filter mentors and send a connection request directly from their profile.
How do I book a session?
Visit your mentor's profile and click Book a Session. Choose a time slot that works for both of you and confirm the booking.
How do I track my goals?
Head to your Dashboard where you'll find a goals tracker. You can add new goals, mark milestones, and view your progress over time.
How do I join the community?
Click on Encouragement or Experiences in the navigation. From there you can join discussions and connect with peers.
How do I update my profile?
Click your avatar in the top right and select My Profile, or go to Settings to update your photo, bio, skills and preferences.
